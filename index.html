THE 500 GSM ANATOMY

Most commercial hoodies use 280–350 GSM polyester blends that pill, lose their shape, and collapse. We designed our custom 500 GSM loopback French Terry from the fiber up.

GARMENT CARE & ORDER POLICIES

Machine wash cold (30°C / 86°F) inside out with mild detergent. Hang or lay flat to air dry. Do not tumble dry on high heat to preserve the dense French Terry loopback texture. Since our garments are pre-shrunk, shrinkage is virtually zero.

Drawstrings are the first element to fray, dangle unevenly, or ruin clean lines. Our hood is constructed with a rigid 2-ply double layer of 500 GSM French Terry that naturally stands upright around your neck for a structured, architectural streetwear look.

We ship worldwide via DHL Express. Orders over $150 receive complimentary Express Shipping. Delivery takes 3–5 business days in North America & Europe, and 4–7 business days elsewhere. We offer a 30-day hassle-free return and exchange guarantee on unworn pieces.

Each batch is limited to 300 pieces per colorway. Core shades (Washed Black and Concrete Grey) restock seasonally, but limited shades (Raw Mocha and Sage Olive) are retired once sold out. Join our VIP Early Access below to guarantee your size.
