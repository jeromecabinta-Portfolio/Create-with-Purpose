The Purpose Vault 8-Point Deadstock Guarantee

Purpose Vault operates an authorized, temperature-controlled archival facility. Our authenticators have inspected over 500,000+ pairs of Nike, Jordan, ANTA, and Converse footwear.

1. High-Intensity UV Blacklight Inspection

We illuminate upper stitching, soles, and footbeds to inspect invisible factory production stamps, glue bleed tolerances, and dye luminescence.

2. RFID & Serial Microchip Scan

Box barcodes and inner shoe tongue tags are cross-referenced directly against Nike SNKRS and ANTA official production batch databases.

3. Gram-Level Precision Scale Check

Each shoe is weighed to within 0.1g tolerance to verify carbon fiber Flyplates, ZoomX foam compound density, and NitroEdge supercritical foam purity.

4. Stitch Density & Font Geometry

Swoosh placement, Jumpman embroidery, KAI glyph runes, and heel tabs are inspected under high magnification for exact thread count.

Tamper-Proof NFC Purpose Vault Tag Attached

Each pair ships with a custom serialized NFC vault security zip-tie. Tap your smartphone to the tag to view the digital Certificate of Authenticity and inspector timestamp.
